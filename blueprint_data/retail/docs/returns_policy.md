# Returns, exchanges and refunds

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery with the receipt or order number, in original condition. Exchanges for a different size or color follow the same window; final-sale and opened personal-care items are not returnable.

Refunds go back to the original payment method after the return is received and inspected, usually within 5-7 business days. Store credit is offered when there is no receipt. Price adjustments and policy exceptions are handled by customer care, not by chat.
