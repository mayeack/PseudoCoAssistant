# Order status and delivery

An order moves from placed to processing to shipped; the tracking link appears in the shipping confirmation email once the carrier scans the package. Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days after shipping.

If tracking shows delivered but the package is missing, check with neighbors and the building's mailroom and wait one business day before opening a claim. Damaged items should be photographed, kept with their packaging, and reported through customer care for a replacement or return.
