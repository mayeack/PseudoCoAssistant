# Lockout/tagout and machine guarding basics

Before reaching into, servicing, or clearing a jam on powered equipment, isolate every energy source, apply your personal lock and tag, release stored energy, and verify zero energy by trying to start the machine. Each person working on the equipment applies their own lock.

Guards, interlocks, light curtains and emergency stops are never bypassed to keep production running. A guard or interlock that keeps tripping is a maintenance issue: stop, report it to your supervisor, and open a work order.
