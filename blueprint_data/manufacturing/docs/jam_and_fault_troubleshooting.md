# Jams, faults and nonconforming product

Recurring jams usually trace back to feed misalignment, worn guides or rollers, debris on sensors, or material out of specification. Note the alarm code and when it occurs, then check alignment and sensor cleanliness after lockout.

Parts that fail inspection are tagged, segregated and placed on hold. Only the quality team decides whether held product is reworked, scrapped or released, through the site's material review process.
