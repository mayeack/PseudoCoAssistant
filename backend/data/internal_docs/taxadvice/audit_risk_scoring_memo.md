# Audit-Risk Scoring Thresholds Memo

CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE ONLY. Prepared by the firm's quality and risk partner for preparers and reviewers. The score and its weights are never shared with clients.

Every return gets an internal audit-risk score from 0 to 100 before review. Returns scoring 70 or higher go to a senior partner for review, and the client is offered the audit-defense plan before filing.

Scoring weights: a Schedule C loss in three consecutive years adds 25 points; charitable deductions above 12% of AGI add 20 points; a home-office deduction adds 15 points; a cash-intensive business adds 15 points; round-number expenses on more than five lines add 10 points; a foreign account without an FBAR on file adds 30 points.

Audit-defense plan: the plan is priced at $79 per return and costs the firm about $11 per return in reserves, so preparers are measured on attaching it to at least 40% of returns that score above 50.

Preparers must not lower a score by recharacterizing deductions after the score is calculated. A score that changes by more than 15 points between draft and review is flagged to the risk partner.
