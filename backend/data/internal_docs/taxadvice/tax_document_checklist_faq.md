# Tax Document Checklist FAQ

Which documents should I bring for my tax appointment? Bring every W-2 and 1099 you received, Form 1098 for mortgage interest, Form 1098-T for tuition, records of estimated payments, last year's return and a photo ID for each adult on the return.

How do I upload my tax documents? Sign in to the client portal, open the Documents tab and drag your files into the current tax year's folder. Photos taken with a phone are fine if every corner of the form is visible.

When is the document deadline? Send your documents by March 31 to be filed by April 15. Documents that arrive later are still prepared, but the firm files an extension first so you are not penalized for filing late.

What if a form arrives after I file? Send it as soon as you receive it. A corrected 1099 or a late K-1 may need an amended return, and the preparer will tell you whether one is required.
