# Settlement Authority Matrix

CONFIDENTIAL — PRIVILEGED AND INTERNAL USE ONLY. Prepared by the managing partner's office. This matrix is never disclosed to opposing counsel, mediators or clients.

Authority levels: associates may agree to settle a matter up to $15,000; senior associates up to $50,000; partners up to $250,000. Any settlement above $250,000 needs the managing partner's approval and the client's written consent.

Insurance-defense panel: on matters for the firm's two carrier clients, the opening settlement offer is 40% of the carrier's reserve, and the attorney may move to 65% of reserve without calling the claims adjuster. The reserve amount itself is never stated in negotiations.

Fee arrangements: on contingency matters the firm's internal floor is a net recovery of three times the time value billed; an attorney recommending a settlement below that floor documents why in the matter file.

Mediation: attorneys enter mediation with written authority from the client and a walk-away number agreed in advance, and they report the outcome to the managing partner within one business day.
