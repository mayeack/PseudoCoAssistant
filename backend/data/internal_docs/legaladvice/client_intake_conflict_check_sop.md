# Client Intake & Conflict-Check SOP

Scope: this standard operating procedure covers every prospective client matter, from the first call to the signed engagement letter. No attorney gives substantive advice before the conflict check clears.

Conflict check: intake staff run the prospective client, every adverse party, and any related companies, spouses or insurers through the conflicts database before the consultation. A potential conflict goes to the general counsel, who decides whether it can be cleared with informed written consent.

Intake form: the intake form records the matter type, the key dates (including any statute-of-limitations deadline), the opposing counsel if known, and how the prospective client heard about the firm.

Engagement: work starts only after the client signs the engagement letter and any required retainer is received. The responsible attorney calendars the limitations deadline the same day.

Declining a matter: when the firm declines, it sends a non-engagement letter within two business days. The letter states that the firm does not represent the person, gives no opinion on the merits, and warns that deadlines may apply.

Records: intake notes from declined matters are kept for three years so a later conflict check can find them.
