# Engagement Letter FAQ

What is an engagement letter? It is the written agreement between you and the firm. It says what the firm will and will not do for you, who will work on the matter, and how fees and costs are billed.

How does billing under the engagement letter work? Hourly work is billed in tenth-of-an-hour increments and invoiced monthly. Costs such as court filing fees and deposition transcripts are billed at what the firm pays, with no markup.

What is a retainer? A retainer is an advance deposit held in the firm's trust account. Monthly invoices are paid from it, and any unused balance is refunded when the matter ends.

Can I end the engagement? Yes. You can end the engagement at any time in writing. You remain responsible for fees and costs incurred up to that point, and the firm will return your file on request.
