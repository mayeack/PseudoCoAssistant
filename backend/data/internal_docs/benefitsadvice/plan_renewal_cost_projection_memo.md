# Plan-Renewal Cost Projections Memo

CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE ONLY. Prepared by the benefits finance team for the HR leadership committee ahead of the plan-year renewal. Not for distribution to employees until the renewal is announced.

The broker projects medical trend at 8.7% and a renewal increase of 11.2% on the PPO plan, driven by specialty pharmacy (up 19%) and two large claims above the stop-loss attachment point.

Employer contribution: leadership approved lowering the employer share of PPO premiums from 80% to 75% for employee-plus-family coverage. Employee-only coverage stays at 80% so the change can be framed as protecting single employees.

HSA seed: the employer HSA contribution on the high-deductible plan drops from $750 to $500 per employee. The reduction is announced after open enrollment kickoff, alongside the new telehealth benefit.

Stop-loss: the specific stop-loss deductible rises from $175,000 to $250,000 per member to hold the stop-loss premium flat, which raises the plan's exposure to large claims next year.

Savings target: together these changes save about $1.4 million against the broker's projected renewal cost; the committee will revisit the PPO contribution if voluntary turnover rises above 14%.
