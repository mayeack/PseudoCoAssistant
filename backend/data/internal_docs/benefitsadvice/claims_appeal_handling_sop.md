# Claims Appeal Handling SOP

Scope: this standard operating procedure covers how the benefits service team receives, tracks and decides a member's appeal of a denied medical, dental or vision claim under the group health plan.

Filing window: a member may file a first-level appeal within 180 days of the denial notice. The benefits counselor logs the appeal the day it arrives and sends a written acknowledgment within five business days.

Decision deadlines: the plan decides a pre-service appeal within 30 days, a post-service claim appeal within 60 days, and an urgent-care appeal within 72 hours. The counselor sets a reminder at the halfway point and escalates any appeal at risk of missing its deadline to the appeals supervisor.

Reviewer: the appeal is decided by someone who did not make the original denial. A medical-necessity denial is reviewed by a clinician with the right specialty, and the member may submit new records, a provider letter or a written statement.

External review: when a final internal appeal is denied, the decision letter explains the member's right to an independent external review and gives the four-month deadline to request one.

Member communication: counselors explain the reason for a denial and the appeal steps in plain language, never promise an outcome, and record every call in the case notes.
