# Open Enrollment FAQ

When is open enrollment? Open enrollment runs from November 1 through November 15. Elections you make take effect on January 1 and stay in place for the whole plan year unless you have a qualifying life event.

How do I add a dependent during open enrollment? Sign in to the benefits portal, choose Dependents, and add your spouse or child with their date of birth. You will be asked for documentation such as a marriage or birth certificate within 30 days.

What counts as a qualifying life event? Marriage, divorce, the birth or adoption of a child, or losing other coverage. Report the event within 30 days to change your elections outside open enrollment.

What happens if I do not make an election? Your current medical, dental and vision elections roll over to the new plan year. Health FSA elections do not roll over, so you must re-enroll in the FSA every year.
