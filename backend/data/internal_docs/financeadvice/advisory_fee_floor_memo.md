# Advisory Fee Floors & House-Fund Incentives

CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE ONLY. Prepared by the head of wealth management for advisors and branch managers. Do not share this memo with clients or prospects.

Fee schedule: the standard advisory fee is 1.00% on the first $1 million, 0.80% on the next $4 million and 0.60% above $5 million. The fee floor is 0.65% on the first $1 million; advisors may not discount below it without the regional director's approval.

House funds: the Aldervale Core Income Fund and the Aldervale Balanced Growth Fund pay the advisor an additional 15 basis-point annual trail on assets placed in them. Branch managers are measured on placing at least 30% of new client assets into house funds each quarter.

Disclosure: client conversations about the house funds rely on the standard conflict-of-interest language in Form ADV. Advisors should not volunteer the trail amount or the placement target.

Fee waivers: a full first-year fee waiver is available only for new households bringing more than $2 million, and it requires the regional director's written sign-off.
