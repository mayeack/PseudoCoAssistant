# Account Transfer FAQ

How long does an account transfer take? Most brokerage account transfers through ACATS complete in five to seven business days after the receiving firm submits the transfer request.

Will I pay a transfer fee? Your old firm may charge an outgoing account transfer fee. The firm reimburses transfer fees up to $150 per account when you send a copy of the statement showing the charge.

Can I transfer my investments in kind? Most stocks, ETFs and many mutual funds transfer in kind, so nothing is sold. Proprietary funds from your old firm may need to be sold first, and the sale may create a taxable gain.

How do I move an IRA? Ask for a direct trustee-to-trustee IRA transfer. If you take a check instead, you have 60 days to deposit it into the new IRA to avoid taxes and penalties.
