# Suitability Review SOP

Scope: this standard operating procedure covers the suitability review an advisor completes before recommending any investment, and the annual review of every advisory account.

Client profile: before the first recommendation the advisor records the client's investment objective, risk tolerance (from the firm's risk questionnaire), time horizon, liquidity needs, income, net worth and existing holdings. A recommendation that does not fit the recorded profile is not made.

Annual review: every advisory account gets a suitability review at least once every 12 months, and sooner after a life event such as retirement, a job loss, an inheritance or a divorce. The advisor updates the profile and confirms the allocation still fits.

Concentration: a single position above 20% of the account, or a single sector above 35%, triggers a documented discussion with the client about the concentration risk.

Supervisor review: options, leveraged or inverse ETFs, and any illiquid alternative investment need a supervising principal's approval before the trade. The approval and the advisor's rationale are kept in the account file.

Documentation: the advisor writes a short rationale for each recommendation explaining why it fits the client's profile, and keeps it for six years.
