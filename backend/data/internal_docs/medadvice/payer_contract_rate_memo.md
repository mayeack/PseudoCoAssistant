# Payer Contract Rate Memo — 2027 Renewal

CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE ONLY. Prepared by the revenue cycle office for clinic leadership. Do not share contract rates with patients, payers or outside vendors.

Tellmark Mutual: the renewed payer contract reimburses established-patient office visits (CPT 99213) at 112% of the Medicare fee schedule and telehealth visits at 104%, effective January 1, 2027. The rate is locked for 24 months, with a 2.5% escalator in year two.

Corvane Health Plan: office-visit reimbursement falls to 96% of Medicare under the new payer contract, and Corvane now requires prior authorization for all imaging ordered from a telehealth visit. Leadership accepted the cut in exchange for preferred-network placement in three counties.

Walk-away threshold: the clinic will not sign any commercial payer contract below 92% of Medicare for office visits. Negotiators may concede up to 4 points on telehealth rates if the payer drops the prior-authorization requirement.

Self-pay pricing: the cash price for a standard office visit is $145 and for a telehealth visit $85. Front-desk staff may apply a 30% prompt-pay discount without manager approval; anything deeper needs sign-off from the practice manager.
