# Patient Portal FAQ

How do I reset my patient portal password? Select "Forgot password" on the portal sign-in page and enter the email address on file. The reset link expires after 30 minutes; if it has expired, request a new one rather than reusing the old link.

How do I see my test results in the patient portal? Lab results appear in the Results tab once the ordering clinician has reviewed them, usually within two business days. Imaging reports can take up to five business days.

How do I message my care team? Use the portal's Messages tab and pick the clinician or the nurse line. Messages are answered within one business day. Do not use portal messages for urgent symptoms; call the nurse line or 911 instead.

Can a family member access my patient portal? Yes, with proxy access. Submit the proxy authorization form at the front desk or through the Forms tab. Proxy access for a minor child ends automatically at age 12 unless it is renewed.
