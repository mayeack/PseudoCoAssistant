# Device Upgrade FAQ

When am I eligible for a device upgrade? You can upgrade once you have paid off 50% of your current device installment plan, or at any time by paying the remaining balance.

Is there a device upgrade fee? A one-time $35 upgrade fee applies to each new device and appears on your next bill. The fee is waived when you trade in an eligible device.

How does trade-in work? Your trade-in device is valued at the time of purchase. The credit is applied over the length of your new installment plan as long as the trade-in arrives in working condition within 30 days.

Will I keep my phone number after an upgrade? Yes. Your number, plan and voicemail move to the new device. Back up your photos and contacts before you return your old device.
