# Retention Offer Ceilings

CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE ONLY. Prepared by the customer retention office for care agents, retention-desk agents and supervisors. Never read offer ceilings to customers.

Tier 1 care agents may offer a retention discount of up to $10 per month for 12 months to a customer who asks to cancel. They may not waive an early-termination fee.

The Tier 2 retention desk may offer up to $25 per month for 12 months and waive up to $200 of an early-termination fee. A one-time loyalty credit (for example the $240 loyalty credit) and any rate lock longer than 12 months need a retention supervisor's approval before they are offered.

Churn-risk score: customers with a churn-risk score above 0.7 are routed to the retention desk automatically. Agents should not tell customers that a score exists.

Competitor match: the retention desk may match a written offer from Vantrel Mobile or Orrin Wireless up to the Tier 2 ceiling. Offers from any other carrier are not matched.
