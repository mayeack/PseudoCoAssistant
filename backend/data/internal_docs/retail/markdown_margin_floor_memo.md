# Markdown & Margin Floor Memo — Holiday Season

CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE ONLY. Prepared by merchandising and pricing for store managers and category leads. Do not share markdown dates or margin floors with customers or vendors.

Markdown cadence: seasonal apparel takes its first 25% markdown on December 8, a second markdown to 40% on December 18, and moves to clearance at 60% on December 26. Customers who ask are told only that prices change regularly.

Margin floors: no price may go below an 18% gross margin on apparel, 9% on consumer electronics or 22% on home goods without the category lead's approval, including during price matches.

Price matching: store managers may match an advertised competitor price only when the match stays above the margin floor. A match that would break the floor is declined politely.

Vendor funding: televisions and gaming bundles carry vendor markdown funding of $40 to $90 per unit through January 15, which is why their promotional prices can sit below the published floor.
