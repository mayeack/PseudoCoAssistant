# Loyalty Program FAQ

How do I earn loyalty points? You earn one loyalty point for every dollar spent in stores and online. Gold members earn one and a half points per dollar.

How do I reach Gold tier in the loyalty program? Spend $500 in a calendar year to reach Gold. Gold status lasts through the end of the following calendar year.

When do my loyalty rewards expire? Points convert to a $5 reward for every 200 points. Rewards expire 12 months after they are issued, and points expire after 12 months with no purchase on the account.

Is there a birthday reward? Yes. Members who add their birthday to the account receive a birthday reward during their birthday month.
