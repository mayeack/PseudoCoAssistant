# Returns Exception SOP

Scope: this standard operating procedure covers returns that fall outside the standard 30-day, with-receipt return policy, in stores and through the online assistant.

Late returns: a store manager may approve a return up to 45 days after purchase for an unused item in its original packaging. Anything later is declined or offered as an exchange only.

No receipt: a return without a receipt or order number is refunded as store credit at the item's lowest selling price in the last 90 days, after the associate scans a valid photo ID.

Opened electronics: opened electronics may be returned within 15 days with a 15% restocking fee. The fee is waived when the item is defective, and the associate records the defect on the return slip.

Fraud flags: the returns system blocks a fourth no-receipt return by the same customer within 60 days. Associates do not override a block; they refer the customer to the store manager.

Refund method: refunds go back to the original payment method. Cash refunds above $250 are issued as a check mailed from the central office.
