# Preventive Maintenance FAQ

How often is preventive maintenance scheduled? Each machine has a preventive maintenance schedule in the CMMS. Most conveyors and fillers get a lubrication and inspection every 250 run hours and a full PM every quarter.

How do I submit a maintenance work order? Open the CMMS on the line terminal, choose New Work Order, pick the asset and describe the problem. Mark it as a safety issue when there is any risk to people, and the maintenance supervisor is paged.

What happens when a preventive maintenance task is overdue? A PM that is more than seven days overdue is escalated to the maintenance supervisor, and a PM more than 14 days overdue is reviewed in the daily production meeting.

Can operators do preventive maintenance? Operators do the daily autonomous-maintenance checks (cleaning, visual inspection and gauge readings). Tasks that need tools or lockout/tagout are done by maintenance technicians.
