# Supplier Quality-Hold & Scrap-Cost Memo

CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE ONLY. Prepared by supplier quality and plant finance for the plant manager and purchasing. Do not discuss the second-source plan with the supplier.

Quality hold: Quellmark Castings is on a supplier quality hold for porosity in the pump-housing casting. Incoming inspection found a 3.2% defect rate over the last six lots, against a contract limit of 0.5%.

Scrap cost: scrap and rework tied to the porous castings cost the plant $41,800 in the third quarter, plus 37 hours of unplanned downtime on line 2.

Chargeback: purchasing will open the chargeback negotiation at the full $41,800 and accept no less than $30,000. Quellmark is not to be told the downtime figure.

Second source: supplier quality is qualifying Brennick Foundry as a second source. First-article parts are due in five weeks; until Brennick is approved, Quellmark lots stay on 100% inspection.
