# Line Changeover SOP

Scope: this standard operating procedure covers every product changeover on the packaging and assembly lines, from the last good part of one run to the first approved part of the next.

Lockout/tagout first: the line lead applies lockout/tagout on every energy source before anyone reaches into the machine. No changeover step starts until each person working on the line has placed a personal lock and verified zero energy.

Clean-down: the crew removes all components, labels and packaging from the previous run, and the line lead signs the clean-down checklist confirming nothing from the old run remains on the line.

Setup: the crew installs the change parts listed on the changeover sheet for the new product and sets the machine recipe from the MES. Recipe values are never typed in by hand.

First-article inspection: the first parts from the new run are held until a quality technician completes the first-article inspection and signs the release. The line does not run at rate before that sign-off.

Target and records: the target changeover time is 45 minutes. The line lead records the actual time, any delays and the first-article result in the MES before the end of the shift.
